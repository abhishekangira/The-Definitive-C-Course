# src/progress/: the progress document, gating and the streak

These notes also cover the components that read the document: `LocalTask` (`src/interactions/`) and `StreakChip`, `StreakWarrior` and `WarriorArt` (`src/components/`). Untrusted-input rules for imports and pasted codes are in `src/AGENTS.md`.

- **Progress schema** (`store.ts`): one JSON document at `tdcc:v1:progress` (`version, seed, cards, lessons, samples, choices, compilerErrors, exercises, quizzes, projects, settings, streak`). Gating rules live in `gating.ts`. A new field must be added to the `migrate` whitelist, to `mergeProgress`, to the round-trip test in `store.test.ts` and to the exact key list in `src/untrusted-input.test.ts`, or an import silently drops it.
- **Prediction verdicts.** A sample may store `verdict` and `predict64Verdict` (`exact`, `close` or `wrong`). `migrate` keeps only those three and drops anything else, including on every page load. Progress saved before verdicts has neither field: `sampleVerdict` and `sample64Verdict` read `correct` and `predict64Correct` as `exact` or `wrong`, never as `close`. `correct` stays true for an exact prediction and for a close one. The lesson card recomputes the verdict from the saved prediction text, so a token-close prediction saved when `correct` meant exact shows yellow even though `sampleVerdict` reads it as wrong. `mergeProgress` copies a sample whole, so the verdict rides along, except `first`/`first64`: when both documents have the id, those are the worse of the two sides' first tries, read with `firstSampleVerdict`/`firstSample64Verdict` and left out when the kept entry's own answer already implies them (a legacy entry without `first` counts as its current answer, so a legacy miss merged with a newer exact `first` stays a miss). Otherwise the later `at` wins by `Date.parse` (not string order: `2026-01-10T00:00:00Z` is greater as text than `2026-01-10T00:00:00.000Z` and is the same instant; an offset can sort earlier and happen later) and a tie keeps the first document's sample. `migrate` drops a sample whose `at` is not an ISO-8601 datetime — `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss` with an optional `.sss`, then `Z` or `±HH:MM`, the shape `toISOString` writes — or parses as NaN (month 13 and second 60 match that shape; `Date.parse` of them is NaN, and NaN would never lose a merge). A stamp more than 24 hours ahead of the clock that loads it is clamped to `new Date(now + 24h).toISOString()`, not dropped. Exactly 24 hours ahead is kept as written, so an offset form is not rewritten. The 24 hours is skew between devices. Dropping the sample would delete the prediction and a `cleared` entry on every load and Drive pull, and the next merge could bring back what Predict again removed; the clamp still stops a far-future stamp winning every merge, and a later real stamp overtakes the clamped entry. A hand-edited `9999` is still dropped. Drive reconcile imports through `migrate` and then calls `mergeProgress`. "Predict again" stores `{ prediction: '', correct: false, cleared: true, at }` instead of deleting the sample, and `migrate` keeps `cleared` only when it is exactly `true`. `CodeSample` treats a cleared entry as no prediction. `sampleVerdict` does not look at `cleared`, so a reader that counts attempts has to skip cleared entries. The 64-bit check and "Predict the 64-bit output again" set `at` to now; the second removes only `predict64`, `predict64Correct` and `predict64Verdict`.
- **Previewing an unreleased module** (`?preview=m03`, `src/AGENTS.md`): the store's key becomes `tdcc:v1:progress-preview`. `load()` merges that document over the real one with `mergeProgress` (so the real learner seed and earlier modules count), and every write, Settings import, merge and reset goes to the preview key only; the real `tdcc:v1:progress` is never written, so nothing from a half-built module reaches it. Settings and the export date chosen in the preview are laid back over the merge (`mergeProgress` alone would take the real ones), and `replaceProgress` on an ordinary page (Settings reset or import) also deletes the preview document, which holds a copy of the old progress. While previewing, Settings export, import and reset are off (`previewing` in `store.ts`, `SettingsPage`, and the export reminder in `Layout`), and anything new that sends the document out of the page must check `previewing` and stay off too, or it carries draft progress into the real progress elsewhere (Drive sync does: `syncNow` in `drive.ts` returns first thing, and `DriveSyncPanel` shows a note instead of its buttons). `store.preview.test.ts`, `drive.preview.test.ts` and `e2e/unreleased-modules.spec.ts` cover it.
- **Google Drive sync** (`drive.ts`, UI in `src/components/DriveSyncPanel.tsx`, started from `main.tsx`). Opt-in; hidden unless the build sets `VITE_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` (CI passes the repository variable `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`; the owner creates a Web OAuth client in Google Cloud with the authorised JavaScript origins `https://abhishekangira.github.io` and `http://localhost:5173` (`npm run dev`) and the Drive API enabled; `docs/adr/0003-google-drive-sync.md`). Its state (connected, the short-lived token and its expiry, the file id, the last sync, `replaceNext`, the epoch) is its own key `tdcc:v1:drive`, outside the document and shared by every tab. Every sync is pull, `reconcile` (`mergeProgress` with the Drive copy as the base, so its seed wins, and this browser's `settings`), apply, push; `syncOnce` is pure apart from `fetch` and is tested against a fake Drive in `drive.test.ts`. Anything that replaces the document instead of merging (Reset, "Replace my progress") must call `replaceDriveOnNextSync()`, or the next merge brings the old progress back; it writes a new `driveEpoch` into the Drive file, and a browser that sees an epoch it has not synced with adopts the Drive copy instead of merging. Tabs: only the tab that made a change autosaves it (`subscribeProgress` passes `'storage'` for another tab's write), and a round stops before merging when the shared settings show another tab's Reset (`roundCancelled`, `replaceNextAfter`). An empty Drive file (created, but the upload into it failed) counts as no copy. The Google sign-in script loads only on pointer or focus over a sign-in button, so the click keeps its user activation for the popup. CI's build has the client id, so the panel renders in e2e: `e2e/drive-sync.spec.ts` stubs Google with `context.route` and skips when `dist/` was built without an id (`VITE_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=x.apps.googleusercontent.com npm run build` to run it locally).
- `update()` in the progress store is a no-op when nothing changed. Keep effects idempotent: an effect that always writes state loops forever (React error 185).
- **LT solution and variants.** Same gate as a browser exercise (`solutionGate`): all three hints, then two distinct source hashes (`src`; `check.mjs` increments `run` every full run and the MAC changes with it, so the same program prints a new code), or one code plus 20 minutes when `activeMs` is already stored. `recordLocalCode` counts distinct `src` values and stores each canonical code once. The lock text never names minutes, because this panel never adds time. "Show the reference solution" sets `solutionViewed`, moves focus to the solution, and shows `solution.c`. A later progress paste says to keep going; it does not repeat that the solution is newly available. "Start the variant" focuses and announces that box (`role="status"`). A variant allows hints 1–2 only and never a solution; its completion token sets the parent's `mastered` unless that variant's solution was viewed. `attempts` counts accepted pastes, not the runner's run index. `migrate` (load and import) and `mergeProgress` lower `substantive` to the distinct `src` count when progress codes are stored, and never raise it, so a document that counted code strings does not stay unlocked. Checkpoints pass `assessment`, which hides hints, the solution and the variant, and a variant completion there does not master the parent (the checkpoint's `onPassed` records the answer). Native projects offer no solution. Lesson 3.1 ships the first LT exercise, `m03-doctor`: Playwright completes it with codes made by `src/testing/token.ts` and the stored seed (`completeLocalTask` in `e2e/lib.ts`, `e2e/local-task.spec.ts`), and `src/interactions/LocalTask.variants.test.ts` drives the variant flow on the fixture `tools/exercises/fixtures/.../lt-free-list`.
- **Streak, spirit beans and Rio.** `streak.ts` keeps only the local `YYYY-MM-DD` days with a submission (plus the longest run); the current streak is derived from them and "today" (`streakStatus`), so there is no timer and it survives export, import and merge. Spirit beans are derived the same way, by replaying the days: one to start, one more on every 7th day of a streak, at most 3; a bean saves one missed day (it bridges the streak but adds no day) and is used up only when it saves it, so a longer gap ends the streak and keeps the beans: the ones that covered its first days come back and those days stop showing as saved (a deliberate refund; Settings explains it, and Rio's streak-ended line says the beans are kept when any are on hand). A submission is a problem check (pass or fail: `useExerciseProgress.recordAttempt`), a valid `LocalTask` paste, a quiz answer or a finished checkpoint; call `noteActivity(p)` inside the same `update` for any new kind of submission. Lesson predictions, hints and the Playground never count. `StreakChip` (header: Rio's head and the days, hidden when no streak runs, dashed while today's submission is missing) and the Settings panel read it. `warrior.ts` maps the current streak to Rio's form (kid, then Warrior at 3, Golden Warrior 7, Golden Mane 14, Crimson Fur 30, Great Ape 50 days; a broken streak is the defeated kid) and the mood (defeated, worried, ready); the Settings gallery keeps every form the longest streak reached. `StreakWarrior.tsx` plays a transformation the first time a higher form is shown and announces it: six beats (stand, the drawn crouch, that crouch with the aura at flicker then blaze, the flash, the new form in blaze). The flash is Rio's shape, not a block: `WarriorArt flash` paints only the crouched `from` sprite's own cells, a core, a ring (the cells just inside the outline) and an edge (the outermost cells): on a dark page, chosen or from the system, a white-hot `#fffdf0` core and ring with a `#fff3a8` edge; on a light page a near-white core vanished into the page, so the core is a warm `#fff1a6` and the ring and edge are amber `#f08c00` (a 2-cell outline). It has no aura, shadow, rim, blink or sweat, and `StreakWarrior.test.ts` checks that every form's flash paints exactly the sprite's opaque cells. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the sequence is not mounted, the new form shows at once, and the announcement still runs. The sequence clears on the last beat's `animationend` or `animationcancel` (only the beat's own event; a child loop's event bubbles and is ignored), on a timeout of the six beats plus one beat (the timer is cleared on unmount), and when the form changes (the sequence is keyed on the target form, so a new rise restarts it); the form last shown is in its own key `tdcc:v1:warrior` (outside the progress document, like the playground draft). Rio is original pixel art, an homage with original names (the client's choice; ripped game sprites or official art could not be published under this public repo's MIT licence): the pixels are ASCII rows in `src/components/warrior-sprites.ts` (one character per pixel, a 96 x 96 grid). `.` is empty (transparent on a sprite, unchanged on a patch) and a space (`ERASE`) paints transparent, so a patch can erase. `FORM_FRAMES` lists each form's loops, derived from the original rows by `tools/rio/derive-frames.mjs` (the ASCII in `warrior-sprites.ts` stays hand-editable). `tools/rio/contact-sheet.mjs OUT`, run against `vite preview` (`E2E_PORT`), writes `contact-light.png` and `contact-dark.png`: every form and mood, each pose frame frozen, plus the closed eyes (the PR contact sheet; never commit the PNGs). Idle is four frames: frame 0 is the original sprite, frame 1 lifts the head and upper torso 1px, frame 2 is that lift plus a hair or fur tip, and frame 3 is the tip on the resting body. A `shift` (`top`, `bottom`, `dx`, `dy`, `fill`) moves those rows inside `composeFrame` before the patches; `fill` paints the vacated row back with the colour that moved (deleting that row alone opens a one-pixel crack). The defeated droop and the worried hair shiver are the same op, so those bands are not copied rows. The kid's defeated pose is its own three-frame loop (the original `kidDown`, a 1px head droop, then that droop with a hair tip poked sideways). Every worried pose is its own three-frame loop: frame 0 is the sprite plus the original worried patch, and frames 1 and 2 shiver only the hair or fur tips, so the sweat drop stays on the face. Under `.still`, the header head draws the first frame of the streak mood's list (`worried[0]` when at risk); the gallery always draws `idle[0]`. It passes no mood. The blink patch is the original eyes. `blinkForIdle` moves it by that idle frame's `shift.dy` (`IDLE_BLINK_DY` is `[0, -1, -1, 0]`, taken from the frames) so a lifted frame's blink sits on that frame's eyes, and `WarriorArt` paints it inside each pose frame. It runs through `composeFrame` first, so a space (`ERASE`) stays transparent; painting the patch raw treats the space as a palette key (`fill` undefined, which SVG paints black). The blink toggles opacity, not visibility: a `visibility: visible` child paints through a `visibility: hidden` pose frame, and the resting opacity is 0 so `.still` and reduced motion (which set `animation: none`) keep the blink shut. The ape has no blink. The breath is a `shift` rather than copied rows, so `gzip -9c` of `warrior-sprites.ts` stays under 13814 with room for a later pose. `WarriorArt.tsx` turns a frame into SVG paths and computes the aura from frame 0 only (four frames: an inner glow, outer flame tongues that shift up in whole pixels, and particles that rise the same way), the dark-page rim, the shadow and the night sky. `<WarriorArt form mood aura="none|flicker|blaze" />` is also a prop of `WarriorPortrait`. Leaving `aura` out is flicker for golden, mane and crimson and none for kid, warrior and ape (`defaultAura`); `aura="blaze"` draws the full fire on every form, including kid, warrior and ape. `bare` still draws no aura (the header and a locked gallery silhouette). #106 should pass `aura` and not redraw sprites. The transformation's crouch (beats 1 to 3) is `crouchSprite(form, frame 0)` in `warrior-sprites.ts`, drawn from the original pixels and cached: rows from `CROUCH[form].cuff` down stay put (feet, so the shadow and the aura's base are not clipped), the trouser band `[hip, cuff)` loses `drop` rows and bows out by up to `splay` px at the knee, and every row above `hip` moves down by `drop`, painted last so the torso stays on top. `WarriorArt` moves the blink and the sweat by the same `drop`. The transformation always crouches the `from` form, so the Great Ape's row is never shown there; it stays so that `crouch` works on every form (for #106). If a form's legs are redrawn, retune its `CROUCH` row and its pin: `WarriorCrouch.test.ts` checks the planted feet, the drop, the knees, the colours and the blink, pins the pixels of each crouch a learner can see (kid to Crimson Fur, with a minimum drop) and checks that the aura and the sweat follow the drop and the crouch has no idle loop. `HEAD_VIEWBOX` and `SWEAT_AT` belong to the original art; the chip draws frame 0, so a later idle frame that lifts the head does not move the crop. Motion is CSS in whole-pixel steps, off under `.still` and `prefers-reduced-motion`. A multi-frame pose sets `figure.multi` and plays that list (`hold2` / `hold3` / `hold4`) instead of the 1px bob, tremble or slump. Colours are fixed hex values. The streak's e2e tests are described in `e2e/AGENTS.md`.

- **Perfect lessons** (`perfect.ts`) count every scored item on its **first try**:
  - each prediction (and each flagged 64-bit question separately), each `<Choice>` quiz question, and each question a `<CompilerError>` asks (the line picked, the option submitted, and the stage when the author set one, all scored on the first Check; `stage="link"` asks no line).
  - A lesson with zero scored items is never perfect; the footer reads `N of N predictions and quiz questions right` (or just the kind it has), and `N of N questions right` once three kinds are mixed, where listing them gets clumsy (`scoredItemsLabel`, `perfectHint`).
  - Samples keep `first`/`first64` (set at the first reveal or 64-bit check and carried through Predict again; legacy entries without them fall back to the current verdict).
  - Quiz answers live in `choices["<lessonId>#<qid>"]` (`{picked, correct, at}`), written once on the first submission; a question with no record counts as not right, so legacy completed lessons with a quiz lose the mark until re-answered.
  - `qid` is `q` plus an FNV-1a hash of the question text (whitespace collapsed; a string in braces reads as the string), assigned by `choiceIds` in `tools/mdx/remark-course.ts`, so adding or reordering questions keeps saved answers on their questions; rewording a question makes it a new, unanswered one, and the same question twice in a lesson gets `-2`.
  - Compiler-error first answers live in `compilerErrors["<lessonId>#<instance>:<kind>"]` (`{correct, at}`), also written once; `<instance>` is the nested sample id or `session:<id>` (the same exercise twice gets `-2`), `<kind>` is `line`, `kind` or `stage`, and `compilerErrorIds`/`compilerErrorQuestions` in `tools/mdx/remark-course.ts` and `src/content-model/compiler-error.ts` assign them to the `ceid` prop.
  - The line, kind and stage first answers are saved together in one update on the first Check, never when a radio is selected (arrow keys check radios as they move through the group); the line's verdict stays hidden until Check. A question with no record counts as not right, so legacy completed lessons with a compiler-error exercise lose the mark until re-answered.
  - The `?frontmatter` module adds the lesson's `choices` (its qids, from `lessonChoiceIds`) and `compilerErrors` (its `<instance>:<kind>` ids, from `lessonCompilerErrorIds`). The loader parses, the lesson compiles: `tools/mdx/lesson-qids.test.ts` compiles every lesson with `@mdx-js/mdx` and the plugin list of `vite.config.ts` and fails when the compiled `qid`s/`ceid`s differ from the loader's ids (a lesson whose ids differ could never be perfect), including the questions a `stage` prop adds; keep its plugin list in step with `vite.config.ts`. A `stage` written as a braced literal (`stage={"link"}`) reads the same to the loader (`attrText`) and the component; any other expression fails that test, because the loader cannot count it.
  - Merge keeps the worse first verdict (legacy-aware, above), the earliest choice and the earliest compiler-error first answer.
  - Nested drill fences are `predict=none` (`nested-predict` content lint).
  - `CodeSample`, `Choice` and `CompilerError` report through `usePredictionShown` after the verdict renders and any browser run settles, so lock-in, imports and reruns cannot trigger the celebration; it fires after the last scored item. `Choice` reports only a submission that newly saved a right first answer, `CompilerError` only a Check that newly saved a right first answer, and `CodeSample` only a reveal or 64-bit check that newly recorded an exact `first`/`first64`, so a retry, Check again, a reveal after Predict again, a wrong answer, or an item already answered in imported progress (`perfect-seen` is not exported) never opens it. `CompilerError` keeps the reporter in a ref and its report effect depends on the tick only, so a new `usePredictionShown` identity never replays an old tick (`Choice` relies on `LessonPage` passing a stable `useCallback`).
  - The dialog's hint names the kinds the lesson scored (`perfectHint`).
  - `PerfectLesson` is a native modal (`showModal`) that stays open until Close or Esc (`cancel`): no click-anywhere, any-key or auto-close. On close it restores the reporting control, else its `[data-interaction]` box, else the lesson container (`fallbackFocus`), never `body`; it adds `tabindex="-1"` to a fallback target that is not focusable, so interaction boxes (`Choice`) carry no tabindex of their own. Its live region is inside the modal and filled after opening.
  - Rio dabs (`dabFrames` in `warrior-sprites.ts`, re-posed original pixels only) on a 2.4 s opacity loop; confetti spawns on an interval over the whole viewport, capped at `CONFETTI_CAP` live pieces and paused while the tab is hidden; both stop on close/unmount. Reduced motion and `still` show the dab hold, a static title and no confetti.
  - **`perfect-seen`**: seen lesson ids live separately in `tdcc:v1:perfect-seen` and are read defensively. Import and "Replace my progress" keep them (an imported lesson already celebrated in this browser must not celebrate again), but "Reset all progress" calls `resetPerfectSeen()` so starting over can earn the celebration again (`SettingsPage.dom.test.ts` checks both paths).
