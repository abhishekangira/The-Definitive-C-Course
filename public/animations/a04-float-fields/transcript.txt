Decode
A 32-bit float is three bit fields: a sign bit, eight exponent bits and 23 fraction bits. The pattern 3F800000 has exponent 127 and fraction 0, so it is exactly 1.

0.1
The float nearest to 0.1 is 3DCCCCCD. Exponent 123 scales by 2 to the minus 4, and the fraction gives about 1.6, so the stored value is 0.10000000149011612, not one tenth.

Sum
In double precision, 0.1 plus 0.2 prints as 0.30000000000000004, and comparing it with 0.3 with double equals is false. Compare floats with a tolerance.
